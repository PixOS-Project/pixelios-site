# Code of conduct

Be kind, be specific, assume good faith. Criticise code and claims, not people. No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
Hardware bring-up is slow and full of dead ends; ask questions, share logs, and say what you tried.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.
Report problems privately through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
